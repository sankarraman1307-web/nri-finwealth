Giving to your children while you are alive: Dutch gift tax exemptions for 2026

1 min readLast checked 31 October 2026Sources listed at the end

The short version

  • EUR 6,908 a year per child tax free in 2026.
  • Once per child: EUR 33,129, or EUR 69,009 for an expensive study.
  • Only one one-off exemption can be used.

In 2026 you can give each child EUR 6,908 tax free in a year. Once per child, a larger one-off amount of EUR 33,129 applies for a child aged 18 up to 40, or EUR 69,009 if the money goes to an expensive study. Only one of the one-off amounts can be used. These figures are from SRA for 2026.

The amounts

One-off for an expensive studyEUR 69,009 in total (not on top of the 33,129)
Other recipients (for example grandchildren)EUR 2,769

The conditions

  • You can use only one of the one-off exemptions, once per child.
  • In the year you use a one-off exemption, you cannot also use the annual EUR 6,908.
  • Study gift: the part above EUR 33,129 must be spent on the study. SRA lists conditions such as study costs of at least EUR 20,000 a year, a notarial deed, and being able to show how the money was used.
  • The one-off exemption for buying a home no longer exists in 2026.

Example

Two children aged 22 and 25. This year you give each EUR 33,129 using the one-off exemption: EUR 66,258 leaves your estate tax free. Later years: EUR 6,908 per child per year, no condition.

To confirm. How the amounts apply when both parents give, the exact timing rules for gifts, and the study conditions (another source describes them less fully than SRA). Check with the Belastingdienst or an adviser before you rely on the study gift.

Frequently asked

How much can I give my child tax free in the Netherlands?

EUR 6,908 a year in 2026, or once a higher amount of EUR 33,129 for a child aged 18 up to 40.

Is there a higher tax-free gift for study?

Yes: EUR 69,009 in total in 2026, but the part above EUR 33,129 must be spent on the study, with conditions.
