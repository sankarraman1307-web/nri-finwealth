How is inheritance taxed if I live in the Netherlands and own assets in India?

1 min readLast checked 31 October 2026Sources listed at the end

The short version

  • Dutch inheritance tax follows where the person who dies lived.
  • Children pay 10% up to EUR 158,669 and 20% above, after EUR 26,230 each (draft 2026 figures).
  • India has no inheritance tax, according to a secondary source.

Dutch inheritance tax follows where the person who dies lived, and covers their assets worldwide. For children the rate is 10% up to EUR 158,669 and 20% above, after an exemption of EUR 26,230 per child. India has no inheritance or estate tax according to secondary sources, but there is paperwork. Both are subject to confirmation.

Example

A parent who lived in the Netherlands leaves EUR 2,000,000 to two children, so EUR 1,000,000 each.

  • Taxable per child: 1,000,000 - 26,230 = EUR 973,770.
  • Tax: 10% of 158,669 = EUR 15,867, plus 20% of 815,101 = EUR 163,020. Total EUR 178,887 per child.
  • For both children about EUR 357,800, around 18% of the estate.

India

According to a secondary source, India has no inheritance or estate tax and receiving property by will or inheritance is not taxed. Income from the inherited property is taxed in the heir's hands, and on a later sale the heir takes the previous owner's cost and holding period.

Ways to reduce the tax

Giving to your children while you are alive, within the exemptions, reduces what is left to tax. See giving to children while alive.
