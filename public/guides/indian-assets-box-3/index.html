Are my Indian assets taxed in Dutch Box 3?

2 min readLast checked 31 October 2026Sources listed at the end

The short version

  • Box 3 covers your Indian accounts, funds and property while you live in the Netherlands.
  • After you move away, you declare only Dutch assets.
  • India may tax the same assets again.

Yes. While you live in the Netherlands, Box 3 covers your assets wherever they are, including Indian bank accounts and property. After you move away and no longer live in the Netherlands, you declare only your Dutch assets. Indian tax on the same assets can come on top.

What the Belastingdienst says

The Belastingdienst says you must declare assets in the Netherlands or abroad. Foreign bank accounts count as bank accounts. A second home counts whether it is in the Netherlands or abroad. Shares count without regard to country. If you live outside the Netherlands, you declare only your Dutch assets, for example a second home in the Netherlands.

How Box 3 works in 2026

  • Tax-free allowance: EUR 59,357 per person (EUR 118,714 for fiscal partners).
  • Assumed return: 1.28% on bank and savings (provisional), 6.00% on other assets such as shares, funds and bonds, and 2.70% on debts (provisional).
  • Tax rate: 36% of the assumed return.
  • Debts count only above EUR 3,800 per person.
  • If your actual return was lower than the assumed one, you can use the counter-evidence scheme in 2026.

Example

You are single and own EUR 100,000 in Indian deposits and EUR 100,000 in Indian mutual funds, with no debts.

  • Assumed return: 100,000 x 1.28% + 100,000 x 6.00% = EUR 1,280 + EUR 6,000 = EUR 7,280.
  • Taxable share: (200,000 - 59,357) / 200,000 = 70.3%.
  • Taxable return: 7,280 x 70.3% = about EUR 5,119.
  • Tax at 36%: about EUR 1,843 a year.

This is a simplified example. It ignores debts, exchange rate moves and special allowances. It is the Dutch tax only: India may also tax interest, rent or gains from the same assets.

Two taxes on one asset

India taxes Indian income (interest, rent, gains) even when you live abroad, and the Netherlands taxes the same assets through Box 3. The treaty's Article 23 is meant to avoid taxing the same income twice. Whether and how it gives relief against Box 3, which taxes an assumed return rather than your actual income, is a question for an adviser. The planner does not assume any relief by default.

To confirm with an adviser. Whether and how Indian tax can be offset against Box 3, and how particular products (NRE and NRO accounts, mutual funds) are treated. Box 3 rules are also due to change, so rates after 2026 may differ.

Frequently asked

Do I have to declare my NRE and NRO accounts in the Netherlands?

Foreign bank accounts count as box 3 assets while you live in the Netherlands. Declare them in your Dutch tax return.

Do I still pay Box 3 after I move to India?

If you live outside the Netherlands, you declare only your Dutch assets, according to the Belastingdienst.
