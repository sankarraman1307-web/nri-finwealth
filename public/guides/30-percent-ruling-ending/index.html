The 30% ruling is ending or dropping to 27%: what it means for your retirement

1 min readLast checked 31 October 2026Sources listed at the end

The short version

  • The ruling lasts at most 5 years.
  • For rulings started after 1 January 2024 it drops from 30% to 27% in 2027.
  • Plan your savings on the pay after the ruling.

The 30% ruling lets your employer pay up to 30% of your salary tax free for a maximum of 5 years. If you started before 1 January 2024, the 30% continues for your 5 years. If you started after that date, it is 30% until the end of 2026 and 27% from 2027. When it ends, your take-home pay drops, so the amount you can save for retirement changes.

The rule in one table

When you started the rulingTax-free share
Before 1 January 202430% for the whole 5 years
After 1 January 202430% in 2024, 2025 and 2026, then 27% from 1 January 2027

From 2027 the minimum salary needed to start is EUR 50,436 (EUR 46,107 for older cases, adjusted each year). Employees under 30 with a master's degree have a lower limit.

Why this matters for retirement planning

  • Your savings rate falls. If you plan to save from your take-home pay, the planner's "monthly savings" should be the number after the ruling ends, not before.
  • The ruling is temporary. Many people plan their whole retirement savings on a salary that exists for only 5 years.
  • Your timeline. If you plan to leave the Netherlands around the time the ruling ends, the ruling's years may be your last years of saving at this level.

Example. When the tax-free share drops from 30% to 27%, three percentage points of your salary become taxable. On a salary of EUR 100,000 that is EUR 3,000 a year more that is taxed at your top rate. When the ruling ends completely, the whole tax-free share goes.

If you enter your 30% ruling in the planner, it shows what the ruling is worth to your household, when your take-home pay drops, and what investing the extra pay could grow to.

Your own start date. The exact treatment depends on when your ruling started and on your employment details. Check with your employer or the Belastingdienst or RVO for your own case.

Frequently asked

How long does the 30% ruling last?

A maximum of 5 years.

Is the 30% ruling really changing to 27%?

Yes. According to business.gov.nl, for rulings that start after 1 January 2024 the tax-free share becomes 27% from 1 January 2027.
