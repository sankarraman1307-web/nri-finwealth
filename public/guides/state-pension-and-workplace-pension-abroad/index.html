The short version

  • You keep what you have built up.
  • You cannot normally cash it out early.
  • Leaving the Netherlands does not unlock it.

You keep what you have built up, but you cannot normally cash it out early. The Dutch State Pension (AOW) is paid from the pension age (67 for 2026 and 2027, 67 years and 3 months from 2028). Your employer pension stays with the fund and is paid at its own retirement age. Leaving the Netherlands does not unlock either one.

The Dutch State Pension (AOW)

AOW is the Dutch state pension. Each year you are insured under the Dutch scheme earns you 2% of a full pension, over the 50 years before your pension age. A person with all 50 years gets the full amount. Each year you are not insured reduces it by 2%. Living or working abroad usually means you are no longer insured.

Example. You lived in the Netherlands from age 30 to 55, which is 25 years, and nowhere else with Dutch insurance. You would build up about 50% of a full AOW (25 years at 2%).

Voluntary insurance. If you leave, you may be able to keep building AOW by insuring voluntarily. You must have been insured at least one full year and apply before you leave or within one year after. It lasts up to 10 years in the standard case and it costs a percentage of your income each year. The SVB has a calculator.

Being paid abroad. Whether the AOW is paid in a given country depends on that country: check the SVB page for India before you plan on it. If you live abroad, apply for AOW six months before you reach your pension age.

Our planner assumes the State Pension starts at 68, a little later than the legal age, to stay on the cautious side.

Workplace pension: can I take a lump sum when I leave?

This is one of the most common questions, and the short answer is no. Leaving the Netherlands does not unlock your employer pension. It stays with the fund and is paid from your pension age.

  • Very small pensions. A pension below a small limit (EUR 632.63 gross a year in 2026) may be paid out as one amount, but funds such as PGB say this is no longer possible when you leave an employer. They try to move it to your new pension provider instead. StiPP allows it only after five years without new pension saving, or within five years of AOW age.
  • Transfer to a pension abroad. Possible only under conditions. Outside the EU, your fund must ask De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) to approve it. For the transfer to stay tax free you need, among other things, at least 5 continuous years of work outside the Netherlands and a foreign scheme that agrees to take it. A transfer into an Indian scheme should not be assumed.
  • New pension system. Dutch funds are moving to a new pension system, and some transfers were paused in the first half of 2026.

Which country taxes your pension?

The Netherlands-India tax treaty (Article 18) says pensions and annuities from past employment are taxable only in the country where you live, while social-security pensions may be taxed by the country that pays them. Government pensions have their own rule (Article 19). The treaty also has a rule to avoid taxing the same income twice (Article 23).

How this applies to AOW, to your workplace pension, and to the paperwork needed so that your Dutch fund pays without withholding tax: ask an adviser before you leave.

To confirm with your fund and an adviser. The general rule that employer pensions cannot be cashed out when you emigrate is widely applied, but the sources above state the exceptions rather than the rule itself. Ask your pension fund in writing. Also confirm with an adviser how AOW and your workplace pension are taxed once you live in India.

Frequently asked

What does AOW mean?

AOW stands for Algemene Ouderdomswet, the Dutch State Pension. It is paid by the SVB to people who have lived or worked in the Netherlands, from the pension age.

Can I take my Dutch employer pension as a lump sum when I leave the Netherlands?

In general no. A small pension below a limit (EUR 632.63 a year in 2026) can sometimes be paid out, and transfers abroad need approval. Check your own fund.

Do I lose my Dutch pension if I move to India?

No. What you have built up stays yours and is paid from your pension age. What changes is that you stop building up new State Pension unless you insure voluntarily.
