Can I retire in India after living in the Netherlands?

The short version

  • Four things decide it: pensions, tax, months in India, and how much you need.
  • Your Dutch pensions are paid abroad, but not early.
  • The planner turns this into a yes or no for your numbers.

Often yes, but four things decide it: your Dutch State Pension (AOW) and workplace pensions, how Indian and Dutch tax treat your money, how many months a year you really spend in India, and what your savings earn. This guide shows where to look. The free planner turns it into a yes or no for your own numbers.

The four things that decide it

1. Your pensions. The Dutch State Pension (called AOW) and your employer pension are paid to you abroad, but they do not start before your pension age and you cannot normally take them out early. See your Dutch pensions if you move to India.

2. Tax. Two countries may want a share. The Netherlands taxes your assets in Box 3 while you live there, including Indian bank accounts and property (Indian assets in Box 3). India taxes you depending on your Indian tax status (NRI, RNOR or resident).

3. Where you spend your months. Spending half the year in each country is a popular plan. It is also the case where tax residency becomes hardest to judge.

4. How much you need. Monthly spending in India is lower for most people, but not for everyone, and inflation in India differs from the Netherlands. See how much do I need?

A short checklist before you decide

  • Ask mijnpensioenoverzicht.nl what pensions you have built up in the Netherlands and from which age they are paid.
  • Check how many years of State Pension you have built up. Each year counts for 2% (details in the next guide).
  • List your Indian assets: NRO and NRE accounts, shares and funds, property, any Indian pension.
  • Decide how many months a year you will really spend in India. Write the number down.
  • Run the planner with a cautious return, then with a lower one.
Your own case. This guide is general information. Your nationality, your Indian tax status and the exact year you move can change the answer. Check the final plan with a qualified cross-border adviser.

Frequently asked

Can I get my Dutch pension in India?

Yes for the pension you have built up, but when it starts and how much it is depends on your State Pension build-up and on your workplace pension scheme. Check with the SVB (State Pension) and your pension fund (workplace pension).

Is it cheaper to live in India?

For many people monthly spending is lower, but it depends on city, lifestyle and healthcare. Enter your own monthly cost in the planner rather than relying on a general rule.
